How it works
  • UPI accepts any bank account as ACCOUNT@IFSC.ifsc.npci. The QR holds that address.
  • Text is read on your device with Tesseract. Images and details are never uploaded.
  • Only the IFSC is sent to Razorpay's public IFSC directory, to show the bank name.
  • Works only if the bank accepts UPI transfers. Some small co-op banks do not.
