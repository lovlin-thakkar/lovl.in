## 2. Verdict: is there a correction right now?

### Takeaway
**PARTIALLY TRUE, and true where it matters most for headline indices.**
- **Large caps (Nifty 50, Sensex): TRUE.** They are in a clear correction, at −14.9% and −16% from their all-time highs. That is beyond the 10% threshold but short of the 20% bear-market threshold.
- **Nifty IT:** fell 11% in September alone and is plausibly near or in bear territory, though its all-time-high data is a gap.
- **Midcaps:** a dip of about 8%, approaching but not yet a correction from their highs.
- **Smallcaps (Smallcap 250):** only about 4-5% off their highs. **Not** in a correction.
- **Overall:** "Correction" is accurate for frontline indices, but not for the whole market.

## 3. Valuations: are Indian stocks "at a discount"?

### Takeaway
On trailing P/E, the **Nifty 50 (about 19-19.7x) and the Nifty Next 50 (about 19x) trade 15-18% below their 5- and 10-year averages**. That is a genuine discount to history, helped by 18% earnings growth in Q1 FY27. Market cap-to-GDP has fallen from 154% (Sep 2025) to about 121%, but is still about 10% above its long-term average. Smallcaps are reportedly still "overvalued." **Net: large caps are cheap vs their own history; small caps are not.**

## 4. Drivers of market moves in 2026

### Takeaway
The 2026 slide is **macro and flow driven, not earnings driven**:
- **Early 2026:** a Middle East oil shock (Q1 2026).
- **Since August:** Brent above $100, US 10-year yields above 5.2%, and the rupee above ₹96/$. This triggered heavy FPI selling and an expected **RBI rate hike on 7 Oct 2026**, the first since Feb 2023.
- **What cushioned it:** Q1 FY27 earnings were strong (Nifty PAT +18%), and domestic SIP flows hit records.
