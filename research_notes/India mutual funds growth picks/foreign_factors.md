# Foreign / Global Factors Affecting the Indian Economy and Equity Markets (as of 3 October 2026)

> Method note: compiled from web searches on 3 Oct 2026. Most primary pages (Business Standard, CNBC, Reuters-syndicating sites) were blocked for direct fetching in this environment, so many figures rest on search-result snippets of the cited articles. Figures were cross-checked across 2+ outlets where possible; conflicts are flagged inline. Treat single-source numbers as "reported", not verified.

**Big picture (one paragraph):** 2026 has been a severe external-shock year for India. A US/Israel–Iran war that began 28 Feb 2026 led to the closure/disruption of the Strait of Hormuz (from ~4 Mar 2026), pushing Brent above $100 (peaks >$120 in spring; ~$107 in late Sep 2026). That shock, combined with (a) the global AI-hardware trade pulling EM money into Taiwan/Korea, (b) a hawkish turn by the US Fed (hike to 3.75–4.00% on 16 Sep 2026) and US 10-yr yields above 5.2%, and (c) a weak rupee (~96/USD), has produced record FPI outflows from Indian equities, FPI ownership at 17-year lows, Nifty down ~13% YTD (end-Sep 2026), and an expected RBI rate *hike* on 7 Oct 2026. Partial offsets: the Feb 2026 India–US interim trade framework cut US tariffs on Indian goods from 50% to 18%, and the $100k H-1B fee is currently blocked by US courts.

## 1. FII/FPI flows into Indian equities (2025–2026), ownership share, and reasons

Selling resumed sharply in September 2026 (≈₹35,900 crore NSDL; ≈₹44,000 crore by exchange data), and FPI ownership of NSE-listed companies is at a 17-year low (15.8% at 31 Mar 2026).

## 2. US Federal Reserve, US yields, dollar, US economy

### Takeaway
The Fed reversed course in 2026. It cut three times in 2025 (to 3.50–3.75%), held through mid-2026, and **hiked 25 bp to 3.75–4.00% on 16 Sep 2026 (12–0)**, its first hike since 2023, with the dot plot signalling one more 2026 hike. US 10-yr yields hit ~5.3% (highest since 2002), DXY ~101–102. But a very weak September jobs report (2 Oct 2026, +29k, unemployment 4.2%) has lowered the odds of another near-term hike.

## 3. US–India trade relations, tariffs, H-1B

### Takeaway
US tariffs on India peaked at 50% in Aug 2025: a 25% reciprocal tariff plus a 25% penalty for buying Russian oil. The Feb 2026 interim framework removed the Russia penalty from 7 Feb 2026 and cut the effective rate to 18%, with zero reciprocal tariffs for gems/diamonds, pharma, smartphones and some agri goods. A full bilateral trade agreement (BTA) is described as "90%+" or "99%" done but **was still unsigned as of early Oct 2026**; Commerce Minister Goyal is in the US on 29 Sep–5 Oct 2026. The $100k H-1B fee (Sep 2025) has been **vacated or blocked by US courts since June 2026**.

### Cited Findings
- 2025 tariffs: the extra 25% punitive tariff over India's Russian oil purchases was imposed in Aug 2025, on top of a 25% reciprocal tariff (peak 50%) — [India Briefing](https://www.india-briefing.com/news/us-india-interim-trade-agreement-18-percent-tariff-42514.html/); [Morgan Lewis, Feb 2026](https://www.morganlewis.com/pubs/2026/02/us-india-trade-deal-cuts-tariffs-eases-tensions)
- 6 Feb 2026: US–India framework for an interim agreement. The White House revised the Russia-oil executive order to remove those tariffs effective 7 Feb 2026, and the effective US tariff on Indian goods fell to 18% — [India Briefing](https://www.india-briefing.com/news/us-india-interim-trade-agreement-18-percent-tariff-42514.html/); [Morgan Lewis](https://www.morganlewis.com/pubs/2026/02/us-india-trade-deal-cuts-tariffs-eases-tensions); [India Briefing timeline](https://www.india-briefing.com/news/india-us-trade-deal-tariff-cuts-timeline-42471.html/)
- Zero reciprocal tariff products: gems and diamonds, pharmaceuticals, smartphones, select agri products, tea, coffee, fruits, handicrafts — [India Briefing](https://www.india-briefing.com/news/us-india-interim-trade-agreement-18-percent-tariff-42514.html/)
- India's reported commitments: halt or significantly reduce Russian crude purchases and shift to US (and possibly Venezuelan) energy; cut tariffs and non-tariff barriers on US goods — [India Briefing](https://www.india-briefing.com/news/us-india-interim-trade-agreement-18-percent-tariff-42514.html/); [Drishti IAS](https://www.drishtiias.com/daily-updates/daily-news-analysis/india-us-trade-deal-2026)
- By ~Jun 2026 effective rates were ~10–18% by product; the US Ambassador said "99% of the deal is done" — [LegalDev](https://legaldev.in/blog/us-tariff-on-india-2026) (secondary source)
- 26 Sep 2026: a senior US official said the deal is "90 per cent plus there" — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/india/story/india-us-trade-deal-90-per-cent-plus-there-says-senior-us-official-ahead-of-key-visits-558000-2026-09-26)

India is pushing for a tariff advantage over competing economies, not just a lower headline rate — [The Statesman](https://www.thestatesman.com/world/india-us-set-to-advance-bilateral-trade-pact-during-piyush-goyals-visit-1503644596.html/amp); [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/piyush-goyal-in-us-negotiations-underway-to-advance-bilateral-trade-agreement); [New India Abroad](https://www.newindiaabroad.com/english/diplomacy/india-to-finalise-us-trade-deal-as-soon-as-preferential-tariff-advantage-is-reached-piyush-goyal)
- **H-1B:** A proclamation (Sep 2025) imposed a $100,000 fee on new H-1B petitions, reportedly extended to 21 Sep 2027. Estimated exposure was >$1 bn for Infosys and ~6,500 employees for TCS. Indian IT firms reportedly cut H-1B registrations by ~92% — [Fortune, 22 Sep 2025](https://fortune.com/asia/2025/09/22/trump-h1b-visa-india-tata-consultancy-services-infosys/); [Whalesbook](https://www.whalesbook.com/news/English/technology/Indian-IT-Firms-Slash-H-1B-Filings-by-92percent-Amid-dollar100000-Fee/6abbd98f5aacb956d085196d) (secondary)
- Dec 2025: a federal judge upheld the fee. **8 Jun 2026:** the US District Court for Massachusetts vacated it as exceeding executive authority under the APA. **24 Jul 2026:** the First Circuit denied the government's stay request, so the fee is not in effect. **30 Sep 2026:** the N.D. California court granted a preliminary injunction in part in another challenge — [Clark Hill](https://www.clarkhill.com/news-events/news/h1b-fee-struck-down-massachusetts-court-2026/); [Clark Hill, First Circuit](https://www.clarkhill.com/news-events/news/first-circuit-blocks-reinstatement-of-100k-h-1b-fee/); [Fragomen](https://www.fragomen.com/insights/united-states-federal-appeals-court-declines-to-stay-lower-court-order-vacating-dollar100000-h-1b-fee.html); [Klasko Law, Aug 2026](https://www.klaskolaw.com/august-2026-100000-h-1b-fee-blocked-again/)

### Inferences
- The tariff shock for exporters (textiles, gems, shrimp, etc.) has largely passed. 18% vs 50% is a big relief, and gems and pharma are at zero reciprocal tariff. The remaining risk is execution: India's ability to keep cutting Russian oil while Hormuz is disrupted, and whether the BTA locks in a preferential margin over Vietnam, Bangladesh and others.
- Signing the BTA during or soon after Goyal's visit would be a modest positive catalyst. Any US accusation that India is backsliding on Russian oil is a tail risk, since July 2026 imports were a record (see Section 5).
- For IT services, the H-1B overhang is legally suspended but not resolved, with appeals pending. The larger IT headwinds are the weak US economy and AI-led deflation in services demand.

- No sector-level export data (textiles, gems, pharma) for 2026 was collected.
- Whether the US Supreme Court's IEEPA tariff litigation changed the legal basis of the India tariffs wasn't researched.
