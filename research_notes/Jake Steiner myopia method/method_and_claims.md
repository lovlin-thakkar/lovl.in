## 2. What exactly is the method? (protocol and terminology)

### Takeaway
The "Reduced Lens Method" asks people to:
- Replace full-strength glasses with slightly weaker ("normalized") distance glasses, about 0.25 D under the 20/20 correction.
- Use much weaker ("differential") glasses for close-up work, about 1–2 D under full.
- Practise "active focus", resolving slight blur into "clear flashes".
- Track progress at home with a centimetre (cm) measurement and Snellen charts.
- Spend time outdoors on distance vision.
- Reduce lens strength stepwise, about 0.25 D at a time, over months.

### Cited Findings
- **Overall framing:** The method is "a free, self-directed method for reducing low to moderate nearsightedness". — [About Endmyopia](https://endmyopia.org/about-endmyopia/). The behavioural approach "rests on two ideas: stopping strain and avoiding hours of close-up focus and over-strong distance lenses". — [How Do You Improve Myopia?](https://endmyopia.org/how-do-you-improve-myopia/)
- **Normalized glasses (distance):** These "should be slightly (0.25 or 0.5 D) reduced from a 20/20 correction". The wiki recommends reducing "no more than 0.25 diopters from 20/20 correction, or 0.5 D from full correction", or 0.5 D for people at "7 diopter+". — [Endmyopia Wiki – Normalized](https://wiki.endmyopia.org/wiki/Normalized); [Wiki – Reduction](https://wiki.endmyopia.org/index.php?amp=&mobileaction=toggle_view_desktop&title=Reduction)
- **Differential glasses (close-up):** "Differentials are glasses for close up." "1.00 - 2.00 (1.50) diopters less than full correction is generally a useful reduced lens power." The wiki says they "prevent hyperopic defocus when using screens up close" and are "essential in preventing axial elongation". It also says "Without differentials, you're on the path to ever increasing prescriptions." — [Wiki – FAQ/Differentials](https://wiki.endmyopia.org/wiki/Frequently_Asked_Questions/Differentials); [Wiki – Differentials](https://wiki.endmyopia.org/index.php?title=Differentials&mobileaction=toggle_view_desktop); [Differential vs Normalized](https://endmyopia.org/differential-prescription-vs-normalized-prescription-considerations/)
- **Differential power by working distance:** You "minimize the prescription to where you can only see as far as your comfortable ergonomic viewing distance" (your screen distance), and "Beyond that… you want to encounter some blur". — [Defined: The Blur Horizon](https://endmyopia.org/frauenfeld-method-key-concepts-the-blur-horizon/)
- **Active focus:** "Active focus is introduced as a way to challenge the eye to get clear focus from a slightly blurred image". The eye "is capable of resolving a small degree of blur when challenged, which provides positive stimulus". In practice it is described as a stare and blink. A brief moment of clarity is called a "clear flash". — [everycalculators summary of Endmyopia terms](https://everycalculators.com/endmyopia-differential-glasses-calculator.html); [Wiki – Blur](https://wiki.endmyopia.org/index.php?title=Blur&mobileaction=toggle_view_desktop); [r/myopia "Does active focus work?"](https://redlib.groet-infra.nl/r/myopia/comments/1u8g4a6/does_active_focus_work/)
  - "Clear flash" wording comes from community and wiki snippets and was not fully read.
- **Print pushing:** "Pushing focus (or print pushing) is primarily a strain-reducing technique." Holding text at the edge of the blur horizon in differentials "reduces the extreme end of the focal range of your eye – and the impact of glasses". — [Is Print Pushing The Best Way…](https://endmyopia.org/is-print-pushing-the-best-way-to-improve-your-eyesight/)
- **Blur horizon:** "the distance at which the image you are focused on, becomes blurry." — [Defined: The Blur Horizon](https://endmyopia.org/frauenfeld-method-key-concepts-the-blur-horizon/)
- **Centimetre measurement:** "Hold a ruler [tape measure] up to your eye, and measure how far away you can still read text without blur in centimeters." The formula is "Diopters = -100/cm measurement". — [Wiki – Measurement](https://wiki.endmyopia.org/index.php?title=Measurement&mobileaction=toggle_view_desktop); [Myopia Calculator](https://endmyopia.org/myopia-calculator-2/); [Diopters Explained](https://endmyopia.org/diopters/)
- **Sequencing:** The site has a page titled "Differential Now, Normalized In 4-6 Weeks". This suggests a staged rollout: differentials first, then a reduced distance prescription. Other topics include a reduction guide, "Guide: Reducing differentials", blur adaptation, and astigmatism-specific differentials. — [Pro Topic: Differential Now, Normalized In 4-6 Weeks](https://endmyopia.org/pro-topic-differential-now-normalized-in-4-6-weeks/); [Guide: Reducing differentials](https://wiki.endmyopia.org/index.php?title=Guide%3AReducing_differentials&mobileaction=toggle_view_desktop); [Wiki – Blur adaptation](https://wiki.endmyopia.org/wiki/Blur_adaptation); [High astigmatism differentials](https://endmyopia.org/how-to-choose-a-differential-prescription-if-you-have-high-astigmatism/)
- **Patience framing:** One page is titled "Myopia Reversal: No Quick Hack, Years Of Small Tweaks". The site offers a free "Basics" video series and "Five Steps To Start With". — [The Jake Is In The Details](https://endmyopia.org/the-jake-is-in-the-details/); [Endmyopia Basics](https://endmyopia.org/endmyopia-basics/); [Five Steps](https://endmyopia.org/how-to-improve-eyesight-five-steps/)
- **Plus lenses:** Endmyopia says "plus glasses are not recommended as part of their protocols". — [Trustpilot company reply](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/endmyopia.org)

### Inferences
- The protocol amounts to deliberate, self-managed distance under-correction plus near under-correction, with users choosing lens powers themselves (often ordered online). Progress is self-measured with the cm test and home Snellen charts rather than clinical refraction.
- The 0.25 D step size is the same as the smallest standard prescription step and similar to the test–retest variability of subjective refraction. Small self-reported "reductions" could therefore come from measurement noise or accommodation changes.

- I could not read the full step-by-step protocol pages, such as the "Start Here" page, the minimum interval between reductions, and outdoor-time targets. They were blocked and only snippet-level detail is available.
- Specific outdoor-hours recommendations were not captured from primary pages.

## 3. What results does it claim, and are any independently documented?

### Takeaway
The site claims:
- About 1 D per year of improvement is "realistic" for consistent users.
- Jake's own -5 D was "entirely eliminated".
- 1.2 million people were "helped".
- 363 cases are documented (2013–2026), with a median start of about -5.00 D and a median reduction of about -1.75 D. 61 of these carry "third-party or official-record" signals.

None of the cases I found report cycloplegic refraction or axial length. The registry itself says it is self-selected and is not evidence that the method reliably reverses adult myopia.

### Cited Findings
- **Rate claim:** "around 1 diopter per year is a realistic rate of myopia improvement for people who measure consistently and apply the habits daily." — [How Do You Improve Myopia?](https://endmyopia.org/how-do-you-improve-myopia/) / [Does EndMyopia Work?](https://endmyopia.org/does-endmyopia-work/)
  - Attribution comes from search extracts. The exact page carrying the figure is uncertain between these two.
- **Marketing headline:** The homepage title is "Improve Your Eyesight - Get Rid Of Glasses", and the paid program is named "BackTo20/20". — [endmyopia.org](https://endmyopia.org/); [join.endmyopia.org](https://join.endmyopia.org/)
- **Registry:**
  - 363 documented cases from 2013–2026.
  - 61 carry third-party or official verification: 49 "reported optometrist or ophthalmologist exam results" and 12 "driving-licence vision restrictions lifted".
  - Median start about -5.00 D; median reduction about -1.75 D.
  - Every case links to its original dated report (email, forum post, Facebook post or video).
  
  — [EndMyopia Case Report Registry](https://endmyopia.org/myopia-improvement-registry/)
- **Registry self-caveats:** It is "self-reported, subject to selection and survivorship bias, and does not prove that the method reliably reverses established adult myopia. It is a case-report registry, not a randomised controlled trial." It "makes no population success-rate claim". — [Case Report Registry](https://endmyopia.org/myopia-improvement-registry/)
- **Example testimonials:** "Paul's Progress: -3.25 Reduced To -2.25 (& eliminated astigmatism)" and "Robin's -4.00 to -2.75". Other categories are "Optometrist Confirmed" and "Student Reviews". — [Paul's Progress](https://endmyopia.org/pauls-progress-3-25-reduced-to-2-25-eliminated-astigmatism/); [Robin's -4.00 to -2.75](https://endmyopia.org/robins-4-00-2-75-improving-eyesight-free/); [Category: Optometrist Confirmed](https://endmyopia.org/category/optometrist-confirmed/); [Category: Student Reviews](https://endmyopia.org/category/testimonials/)
- **Video:** "How I Improved My Eyesight Naturally: Short Video". — [endmyopia.org](https://endmyopia.org/how-i-improved-my-eyesight-naturally/)

### Inferences
- Even the "verified" 61 cases rest on manifest (non-cycloplegic) prescriptions or driving-licence acuity tests. Neither separates a true refractive or axial change from relaxed accommodation, blur adaptation or better acuity tolerance. A lifted licence restriction measures visual acuity, not refraction.
- A median reduction of about -1.75 D from about -5 D is far smaller than "get rid of glasses" marketing implies. The median case still wears roughly -3.25 D.

### Gaps
- No case documented with cycloplegic refraction or biometric axial length (IOLMaster/A-scan) before and after was found. A targeted search returned none.
- No peer-reviewed study of the Endmyopia protocol itself was found.
- Jake Steiner's own pre- and post-method clinical records are not publicly documented anywhere I found.

## 4. What mechanism does Endmyopia claim?

### Takeaway
Endmyopia's model mixes two ideas.
- **Short-term:** "Ciliary spasm"/"pseudomyopia" (near-induced transient myopia) turns into "lens-induced myopia" once full-strength minus lenses are worn, especially for near work.
- **Longer-term:** Near work through full correction creates "hyperopic defocus", which drives axial elongation. Removing that stimulus, plus slight "myopic" blur, is said to let the eye change course.

In 2026 the site itself concedes that adult axial-length reversal "has not been demonstrated in a trial".

### Cited Findings
- **Pseudomyopia to lens-induced myopia:** "Ciliary spasm, also referred to as pseudo myopia or near-induced transient myopia, then becomes lens-induced myopia if you start wearing glasses." — [Wiki – FAQ/Myopia](https://wiki.endmyopia.org/wiki/Frequently_Asked_Questions/Myopia)
- **Lens-induced myopia definition:** "the phenomenon that wearing corrective lenses seems to cause myopia to worsen, typically when lenses are overprescribed or when someone wears distance vision glasses for close-up use"; "Hyperopic defocus is the mechanism likely to be the cause of lens-induced myopia." — [Wiki – Lens-induced myopia](https://wiki.endmyopia.org/wiki/Lens-induced_myopia)
- **Distance correction at near:** "Using a full prescription meant to correct myopia at long distances while focused up-close, will lead to more lens-induced myopia." — [Wiki – Differentials](https://wiki.endmyopia.org/index.php?title=Differentials&mobileaction=toggle_view_desktop)
- **"False focal plane":** The site has an essay framing glasses as creating a "false focal plane". — [The False Focal Plane Vs. Reduced Lens Method](https://endmyopia.org/destroying-your-eyesight-the-false-focal-plane/)
- **Admission on reversal:** "Axial length reversal in adults has not been demonstrated in a trial", and readers are told not to believe anyone who claims it has. "The documented mechanism shows growth is regulated by visual input, not that adult human myopia has been reversed." — [Can myopia be reversed? What the evidence actually says](https://endmyopia.org/can-myopia-be-reversed/) / [Does EndMyopia Work?](https://endmyopia.org/does-endmyopia-work/)
- **Citing studies:** Endmyopia cites studies selectively, for example "Another Study: Glasses Cause Rapid Myopia Progression In Children" and a page on whether undercorrection causes more myopia. — [Another Study…](https://endmyopia.org/another-study-glasses-cause-rapid-myopia-progression-in-children/); [STUDY: Does Undercorrection Cause More Myopia?](https://endmyopia.org/studies-does-undercorrection-cause-more-myopia/)
- **Mainstream position (context only):** Under-correction is not supported. Children under-corrected by 0.75 D progressed faster than fully corrected controls (Chung et al., 2002). Surveyed clinicians rated belief that under-correction slows progression at 2.34/5. — [PMC3223709](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3223709); [PMC7739548](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7739548)
  - The science team should verify these studies directly.

### Inferences
- The claimed mechanism shifts between two levels:
  - **Accommodative/pseudomyopic:** Reversible, plausibly explaining small gains of 0.25–1 D measured without cycloplegia.
  - **Structural/axial:** Hyperopic defocus driving elongation, borrowed from animal and myopia-control literature.
  
  Marketing implies large reductions ("get rid of glasses") that would require axial shortening, yet the site now concedes this has not been shown in adults.
- Using hyperopic-defocus theory to justify reduced near lenses has some basis in mainstream literature (near-addition or defocus lenses for children). Extending it to reversal in adults is the method's own claim.

### Gaps
- I could not access the full long-form mechanism pages ("Why Myopia Happens and Reverses", [end-myopia-home](https://endmyopia.org/end-myopia-home/)) to quote their exact causal chain.

## 5. What disclaimers does Endmyopia publish?

### Takeaway
Endmyopia openly disclaims medical status. Jake is "not a medical doctor or optometrist", and the advice is "unlicensed, unsanctioned, un-endorsed". The site positions the method for low to moderate myopia. Its registry is explicitly caveated as self-selected, not an RCT, and not proof. A limitations disclaimer and a financial-interest disclaimer (lens-company stock) are also referenced.

### Cited Findings
- "not a medical doctor or optometrist"; advice "100% unlicensed, unsanctioned, un-endorsed by the optometry establishment." — [Finding Genius Podcast bio](https://futuretech.findinggeniuspodcast.com/podcasts/vision-quest-jake-steiner-founder-of-endmyopia-org-the-way-to-reverse-myopia-take-your-vision-back/)
- Scope: "for reducing low to moderate nearsightedness"; "nothing about the method requires a purchase to try." — [About Endmyopia](https://endmyopia.org/about-endmyopia/)
- Registry caveats: not an RCT, selection and survivorship bias, no population success rate, "does not prove that the method reliably reverses established adult myopia." — [Case Report Registry](https://endmyopia.org/myopia-improvement-registry/)
- The site "contains a disclaimer about limitations of the EndMyopia method" and a financial disclaimer about lens-company stock ownership. — [About: Jake Steiner](https://endmyopia.org/about-us/) (via search extract; exact text not captured)

### Inferences
- The disclaimers sit uneasily with headline marketing: "Get Rid Of Glasses", "BackTo20/20", "1.2 million helped", and Jake's claimed full reversal from -5 D. Readers who see the sales funnel may not encounter the registry caveats.
